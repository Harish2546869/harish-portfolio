Client project · Graphic design

Dermatouch.

A connected visual language for skincare campaigns and e-commerce—designed to keep the product clear, considered and easy to understand.

ProjectSkincare campaign & commerce visuals
FocusSocial media · Meta ads · Amazon & Shopify A+
RoleGraphic design · Visual direction

The brief

Create a consistent set of product-focused visuals across social media, paid Meta campaigns, banners, product pages, Amazon A+ and Shopify A+ content. Each format has its own gallery in the portfolio archive.

Approach

Keep the product and original packaging at the center. Use clean compositions and clear hierarchy, with separate treatments for social content, paid campaigns, banner creative and long-form commerce pages.

01 / SOCIAL MEDIACarousels & stories ↗02 / META ADSCampaign creatives ↗03 / BANNERSDesktop & mobile ↗04 / AMAZON A+Listing content ↗05 / SHOPIFY A+Product storytelling ↗

Design decisions

Packaging remains intact in the supplied product imagery. Social, ads, banners, Amazon A+ and Shopify A+ each have a separate place, so the work is easy to browse and compare without mixing formats.

Outcome

The supplied folder includes social media carousels and stories, Meta ad creatives, desktop and mobile banners, Amazon listing content and Shopify A+ panels. No campaign performance figures or business results were included in the files.
